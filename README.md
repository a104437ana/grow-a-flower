# grow-a-flower

https://grow-a-flower.vercel.app

grow-a-flower is a focus timer: set how long you want to focus (study/work) and watch your flower slowly bloom as the time passes. When it's done, you're rewarded with a beautiful flower — the fruit of your effort — and an alarm lets you know you're finished. Turn off the alarm, then admire your flower or grow another one.

Only works properly on a computer, not a phone.
